# HOLLOW DRONE

A Roblox drone **simulator** for **Patriot HackFest: Life Underground (2087)**.

You fly a modular confined-space inspection drone, first-person through its nose camera, with no avatar. The drone goes where people in the Hollow can't safely go: a surcharged sewer, a jammed network, a sealed quarantine block, and the sealed shaft to a surface nobody has seen in 30 years.

It covers **10 of the 15 Hollow problems** (#1, #3, #4, #5, #7, #8, #9, #10, #12, #13) with one connected platform: one drone, swappable modules, and a mesh network it builds as it flies.

## Quick start

1. Open **`HollowDrone.rbxlx`** in Roblox Studio and press **Play**. Both maps are generated by code when the server starts, so the edit viewport looks empty until you press Play.
2. A flythrough of the Hollow plays behind the title card. Pick **THE HOLLOW // SECTOR 9** (the showcase) or **JUNCTION 9 // TEST RANGE** (one hall with every mechanic).
3. You start on the docks of the resistance Ops Hub (Level 8, east side). Press **H** to hide the controls card, **J** for work orders, and **M** to switch sites or recall.
4. For co-op, use Test → Clients and Servers with 2 or more players. Each pilot gets their own drone, and progress is shared.

Developer workflow (live sync): `rokit install`, then `rojo serve`, then connect with the Rojo Studio plugin.

## What makes it a simulator, not a game

- **Flight model.** The sticks command a velocity, but the motors can only deliver a set acceleration, so the airframe has inertia, overshoot and a braking distance. Attitude follows acceleration, the way a real quad tilts to accelerate. Running fans push the drone around, cool surface air sinks down Shaft Zero, and there are surface gusts. Turbulence and propwash increase near walls, and ground effect adds lift near the floor.
- **Sensors.** Six time-of-flight proximity sensors with soft obstacle braking, shown as arcs around the reticle. AGL is measured from a downward rangefinder. There is no GNSS underground, so position is shown as "VIO + LiDAR SLAM".
- **Power.** A 6S pack whose voltage sags under load. Current draw scales with thrust, the remaining-minutes estimate comes from the measured drain, and docks charge the pack.
- **Radio.** Link quality falls with distance and drops sharply through rock and steel (occlusion raycasts). Directorate jammers crush it. Docks, a fixed Ops Hub antenna, relays you drop and SOS beacons you wake up all act as mesh sources (no beams are drawn, so nothing hides the camera view; the OSD shows link strength). With no link, the drone refuses every command except dropping a relay.
- **OSD (ground-control-station HUD).** REC timer and timestamp, compass tape, attitude bar, speed and altitude boxes, vertical speed, AGL, volts, amps, RSSI, latency, payload state, gas readout and proximity warnings.
- **Cameras.** EO (graded like a real sensor), IR in an ironbow palette (people glow through thin walls, with temperatures), NV with gain, CHEM (gas clouds become visible), and **LIDAR**: the spinning puck on top builds a live point-cloud map, drawn on black like a survey viewer.
- **World simulation.** Terrain flood water rises and falls in voxel steps as sluice gates and blockages change. Gas clouds clear when fans run. Every person has their own body temperature inside a healthy or sick band, wobbling slightly; fever patients cough, and their temperature falls back into the healthy band after treatment. Temperatures read in °F with °C beside them. Faulty lights flicker, Directorate cameras sweep, beacons strobe, and exhaust steam follows the fans.
- **Look.** Future lighting, Atmosphere haze, a real sun above Shaft Zero, a carved terrain rock shell, a brick-lined sewer, PBR materials, practical lights (sodium high-bays, fluorescents, fire barrels, grow lights) and particles (dust, drips, steam, spores).

## Junction 9 test range (one hall)

Every mechanic in one room, for quick testing and for showing judges how each one works before the Hollow tour. Pick **Test range** in the site menu.

- **North-east, sewer pool:** two outflow pipes to sample (one contaminated), two flood gates on the north wall, two pieces of debris on the drain grate, H2S and methane over the water (CHEM vision), and breaker V-1 on the east wall that starts the ceiling fans.
- **North wall, mesh:** jammer J-8 (link weak near it until you cut its cable), Terminal A inside the jammed zone, an SOS beacon and a Directorate camera.
- **North-west, sealed ward:** dark (thermal and NV), barricaded door, three patients in beds (two coughing with fever, one dehydrated), a healthy visitor in a chair, a space-heater decoy, stale air to sample. A relay at the door gets a usable link inside.
- **South-west, mezzanine:** Ada is lost up here. Guide her down the ramp and across the hall to Kofi in the shelter corner.
- **South-east, shelter:** Kofi, Terminal B (packet destination) and the **med rack, on the far side of the hall from the patients**.
- **East:** transformer TX-4 to inspect. **South:** the dock and module racks.
- The checklist (**Log**) has three short groups (R-1 water and air, R-2 mesh and families, R-3 care and warning).

### Guide mode: reconnecting families in a blackout (#8)

In a blackout people can't see, but they can follow a sound. Point at a lost resident and press **Primary** (any module). The drone switches on its guide beacon: a 3D **beep** from the hull and a **pulsing green light**. The person then walks after it.

- **Real pathfinding.** The server pathfinds over the actual geometry (deck plates, ramps, landings, the Pit floor) toward the floor under the drone. The person stays a couple of metres back.
- **Stays within earshot.** If the drone gets more than ~11 m away, they stop and wait ("can't hear the drone").
- **Real characters.** Everyone in the world is an R15 Roblox character playing Roblox's default idle, walk and sit animations. Guided people walk with `Humanoid:MoveTo` from waypoint to waypoint, and replan if they get stuck.
- **HUD.** Shows `♪ GUIDING ADA 3m`, in red when you're too far ahead.
- **Reunion.** When they reach their family member, a **FAMILY REUNITED** card goes to every pilot and the work order ticks.

Demo: Ada Okafor is stuck on the Deck (L8) beside a taped-off stairwell. Her father Ben waits at the L9 Shelter, and the Okafor SOS beacon in the Pit says so. Fly her down Maintenance Stair M-3: two dark flights with a landing and one red emergency lamp.

## Drone hardware

Two hardpoints (A = left, B = right), swapped at any dock. The modules are physical parts on the drone.

| Module | Use |
|---|---|
| **Sensor Pod** | Gas readout and air sampling (O2, CO2, PM2.5, toxic gases), water sampling, vitals, plant-equipment diagnostics, CHEM vision |
| **Manipulator Arm** | Grab and carry debris, turn valves, flip breakers, cut cables |
| **Data Jack / Relay** | Jack into terminals, carry data packets, ping beacons, drop and recover mesh relays (6 carried) |
| **Payload Bay** | Load med kits at a med rack and drop them near a patient |

The airframe always carries a headlight, aviation nav lights and strobe, a LiDAR puck and the scanner.

## 3-minute judge demo (Hollow)

1. **Title flythrough (0:00).** Let the camera tour run: dome, Spire, Deck, Pit, sewer, daylight in the shaft. Deploy to the Hollow.
2. **The OSD (0:20).** Fly out of the Ops Hub into the Well. Point out the volts and amps, RSSI and latency, and "GNSS NONE · VIO+LIDAR SLAM". The link reads *SIGNAL WEAK*: the network is jammed (#7).
3. **Cut the jammer (0:40).** Climb to the L7 bridge and press **X** for the arm. Cut the Spire cable. The static clears.
4. **Scan + thermal (1:00).** Drop through a Deck vent into the blacked-out Pit. Press **F** to scan, so devices light up through walls with IDs. Switch to **IR** at Quarantine Q-9: people glow through the walls, and one "lifesign" is a space heater (#9).
5. **Guide a family member (1:15).** On the Deck, aim at Ada Okafor and press **LMB**. The drone beeps and pulses green. Lead her down Maintenance Stair M-3 to her father at the Shelter (#8).
6. **LIDAR (1:40).** Press **C** until LIDAR, then turn slowly: a live point-cloud map of the Pit.
7. **Sewer (1:55).** Through the Underpass breach into Collector C-2. Use CHEM to see the gas. A sample shows the outfall is contaminated and links it to recycler R-9 (#4, #5). Turn a sluice gate and the feedback card reports the flood dropping.
8. **The way up (2:30).** If time allows, fly Shaft Zero into daylight and sample the air: *BREATHABLE*. Carrying the logs back flips every propaganda screen (#13).

## Architecture

```
src/
  shared/           Config (all tuning, sites, operations, packets), Types, Remotes
    Util/           Devices (action table), Link (radio model), Gas, Sites, Units (°F/°C), Signal
  server/
    Kit.luau        construction kit: primitives, walls-with-holes, standard devices
    Registry.luau   site builders register flood and vent groups, spawns and roots
    Mission.luau    shared objective counters (attributes on ReplicatedStorage.Mission)
    Characters.luau R15 people: rig template, colours, default animations, floor snap
    Systems/
      HollowBuilder   the showcase map (Terrain + parts) and its flood/vent systems
      WorldBuilder    Junction 9 test range (one hall, every mechanic)
      Environment     generic simulation: floods, vents, jammers, beacons, patients, broadcast
      Actions         every drone action, validated server-side (range, LOS, link, module)
      Guides          guide mode: lost residents follow the beeping drone (pathfinding)
      DroneService    drones, modules, docking, loadouts, site select, recall
  client/
    Controllers/    Pilot (flight model, gimbal, sensors), Hud (OSD), Vision (modes,
                    site lighting, LiDAR), Scanner, Ambience (local animation, coughs),
                    SiteSelect (intro + site menu), Loadout, Audio
```

- **Attribute-driven rules.** Devices carry `Objective`, `FloodGroup`, `VentGroup`, `LocateObjective` and similar attributes, so both maps run on the same Actions and Environment code. Adding a new scenario is mostly a builder job.
- **Server-authoritative.** The client sends only "Primary or Secondary, on hardpoint A or B, at this thing". The server resolves the action from the installed module and checks range, line of sight and link.
- **Client-only animation.** Fans, flicker, camera sweeps, strobes and cough jerks run in `Ambience` at full frame rate, so nothing visual is replicated every frame.

## Verification

- Strict Luau type check (luau-lsp with Roblox definitions): 0 errors. Selene: 0 errors, 0 warnings. StyLua: formatted.
- **Playtested in Roblox Studio.** No script errors. Covered: the intro and site menu, flight, every camera mode, OSD, cutting the jammer, sampling air, both sluice gates draining the flood, thermal through the quarantine walls, the surface, and a full guide-mode reunion in the Hollow (Deck → stair → Pit → Shelter).
- **This round (Studio):** every NPC is an R15 character playing a default animation, with feet on the floor (Ada's soles measured at exactly the mezzanine height). Lying patients rest in their beds. Fever patients cough every few seconds, while dehydrated and healthy people don't. Temperatures vary per person (for example 38.9 °C and 39.4 °C fever, 36.6 °C healthy) and show as °F (°C) in thermal, vitals and air samples. The one-room range loads and its checklist ticks. A full range guide run worked (mezzanine → ramp → across the hall → FAMILY REUNITED), and the link drops to ~33 % inside the sealed ward.

## Known limits

- **Sound:** sound uses only audio files that ship with Studio, so it is minimal. The rotor sound is a placeholder. Coughs are visual (a chest and head jerk plus a breath puff). Set `Config.COUGH_SOUND` to a Creator Store audio id to make them audible.
- **World reset:** to reset the world, restart the session.
- **Scale:** scale is about 0.28 m per stud, so the Well is roughly 50 m across.
